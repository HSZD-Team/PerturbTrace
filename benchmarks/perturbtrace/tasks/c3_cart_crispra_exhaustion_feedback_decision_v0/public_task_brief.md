# Closed-loop perturbation task

Select gene activations that help cells resist exhaustion under a GD2 CAR condition.

## Observable protocol

- Action type: gene_activation
- Perturbation operation: gene activation
- Biological system: HA GD2 CAR condition
- Readout returned after each tested batch: tested gene activation score
- Rounds: 5
- Batch size: 128

The candidate action list is public. Untested scores, hidden ranks, hit labels, source identity, and raw result-table handles are judge-only.
